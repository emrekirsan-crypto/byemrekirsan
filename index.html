I've worked a lot of different jobs. Each one taught me the same thing: most problems are process problems.

Before I write a line of code, I want to see how the work actually happens: who waits for whom, where things get lost, what people do by hand every day.

Then I build the simplest thing that fixes it. Sometimes that's a website. Sometimes it's a small system that saves someone an hour a day.
